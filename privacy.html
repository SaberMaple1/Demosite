Privacy Policy

Pickago Canteen · Effective [date]

Pickago is the canteen ordering website of [School name]. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It is written with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) in mind.

1. What we collect

  • Sign-up details: your email address, your Student ID, your name and your grade & section.
  • Your password, which is stored in scrambled form so nobody (including the admin) can read it.
  • Your orders: what you ordered, when, the pickup day and the order status.
  • Account activity: when you last signed in, and the date you agreed to the Terms and this policy.
  • Optional details you add yourself, such as a phone number or a profile photo.

2. Why we collect it

  • To check that the email address is yours and to create your account.
  • To let you place orders and pre-orders, and to let canteen staff prepare and hand them out.
  • To let the school admin manage Student IDs and accounts, and to keep the service safe.

3. Who can see it

  • You can see your own details and orders.
  • Canteen staff can see your name and your orders.
  • The admin can see your Student ID, name, grade & section, whether your account is active and when you last signed in. The admin cannot see your password.
  • Service providers that run the website process your data for us: the database host (Supabase), the website host, and Google (which sends the email containing your Student ID).

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account until the admin deletes it or until the auto-delete date set for it, usually the end of the school year. Past orders may be kept without your account details for sales records. You can ask the admin to delete your account earlier.

5. How we protect it

Connections to the website are encrypted, passwords are stored scrambled, and the database only answers requests that come with a valid login. No online system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private and log out on shared devices.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to be informed about how your data is used, to see it, to correct it, to object to its use, and to have it blocked or deleted. You also have the right to complain to the National Privacy Commission.

7. Students under 18

If you are a minor, please make sure your parent or guardian knows you are signing up. The school may ask for their consent according to its own rules.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new version here. Questions or requests: [contact name and email].
